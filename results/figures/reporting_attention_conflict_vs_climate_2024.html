Reporting attention over time: conflict vs. climate stress

Mean number of ReliefWeb reports per month mentioning each group's districts, Somalia 2024. Conflict districts (n=18) and climate-stress districts (n=41) are the same two groups used in the extreme-conflict-vs-extreme-climate-stress analysis: districts that were ever in the worst decile of conflict events, or the worst decile of vegetation health, but not both.

Source: vw_food_insecurity_panel.csv. Conflict severity: worst decile of conflict_event_count (ACLED). Climate severity: worst decile of vegetation_health_index_mean. Each month shown is the mean of reports_mentioning_district_count across the group's fixed membership, not just the district-months where that group happened to be at its most extreme that month.
