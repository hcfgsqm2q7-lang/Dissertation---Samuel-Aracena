  - food price and market features.

### Important results or issues

- Demonstrated that no single humanitarian dataset contains all relevant information.
- Identified complementary information across datasets that could be integrated into a unified resource.
- Main uncertainty now is no longer data collection but the scientific contribution and research direction.

### The paper’s contributions would be:

The best framing is not “agreement between news aggregators” anymore.  
I would frame it as: *A Source-Aware Humanitarian Data Panel for Food Insecurity Monitoring in the Horn of Africa*.

1. A harmonised ADM2-month schema integrating event, report, rainfall and price data.
2. A source-provenance framework preserving semantic, spatial and temporal uncertainty.
3. A descriptive analysis of complementarity and missingness across sources.
4. A pilot food insecurity use case using IPC labels.
